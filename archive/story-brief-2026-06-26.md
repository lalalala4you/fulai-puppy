### Story 1: What Training a Chaotic Dog Teaches Us About Life
- **Source:** The Guardian (June 2026 Feature)
- **Why it's hot:** A long-read essay that went viral for its honest, heartfelt take on how raising a "chaotic" dog mirrors the messiness of being human — struck a chord with millions of pet parents.
- **The story:** The writer shares how their difficult-to-train dog — reactive, stubborn, full of chaotic energy — became their greatest teacher in patience, control, and self-awareness. Every training failure revealed as much about the human as the dog. The piece resonates because it flips the script: instead of "fixing" your dog, you learn to meet them where they are.
- **🎬 Fulai Angle:** Fulai is a mixed breed with his own personality — use this as a "behind the scenes" moment. Film a 15-sec clip of Fulai doing something slightly chaotic (chasing his tail, barking at nothing, stubbornly refusing to move), then cut to a warm shot of him calmly sleeping on his bed. Text overlay: "Some days chaos, some days peace. Both are Fulai." This is the kind of authentic, unfiltered content that beats polished perfection every time.
- **Platform fit:** TikTok / Reels (short-form, emotional hook) + RED (longer caption about accepting your dog's true self)

### Story 2: Dog Rescued After Being Swept Off Rocks, Swimming Almost 1km Offshore
- **Source:** The Guardian Australia (June 2026 — includes video)
- **Why it's hot:** Dramatic real-life rescue with video footage — dog vs. ocean, against all odds survival story. The kind of story that gets millions of views because everyone roots for the underdog.
- **The story:** A dog was swept off the rocks by a wave and ended up in the open ocean. Remarkably, instead of panicking, the dog swam for nearly a kilometer until it reached a small island off the NSW coast. Rescuers were able to reach the dog and bring it safely back to shore. The video of the rescue and the dog's sheer determination is what made this story spread.
- **🎬 Fulai Angle:** Fulai is no ocean swimmer, but every dog has their "survivor moment." Ask mom to film Fulai doing something that shows his resilient/problem-solving side — figuring out how to get a toy from under the sofa, finding his favorite spot in the sun, or how he "asks" for food in his own clever way. Caption: "Fulai might not swim a kilometer, but watch him solve the 'treat under the rug' puzzle in 3 seconds flat. 🧠✨"
- **Platform fit:** TikTok (trending sound + rescue narrative) / Reels

### Story 3: "She'd Consumed a Kilo of Sand" — 11 Dog Owners Share the Weirdest Things Their Dogs Have Eaten
- **Source:** The Guardian Readers (June 2026)
- **Why it's hot:** Every dog owner has a "my dog ate something bizarre" story — this kind of shared experience content is the glue of pet communities. Relatable, funny, and highly commentable (everyone wants to share their own).
- **The story:** Guardian readers submitted their dogs' weirdest dietary indiscretions: one dog ate a kilo of sand, another consumed nipple cream and marbles, a third ate an entire bag of flour (the mess was legendary). It's a celebration of how dogs are simultaneously disgusting, hilarious, and completely lovable.
- **🎬 Fulai Angle:** Does Fulai have a weird eating habit? Ask mom for *that* story — the one thing Fulai tried to eat that made no sense. Film a reenactment video: show a normal object (sock, stick, random leaf), cut to Fulai looking guilty, then text overlay: "Fulai's 5-star Michelin review of this random leaf: ★★★★★" It's cheap, easy, and every dog owner will feel seen.
- **Platform fit:** TikTok (fast cuts, trending funny audio) / RED (ask for community participation: "What's the weirdest thing YOUR dog has eaten?")
