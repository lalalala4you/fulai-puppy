### Story 1: "Benny the Marathon Dog" hands out free pets at the Chicago Marathon — again

- **The story:** Benny is a 5-year-old golden retriever who stations himself along the Chicago Marathon course holding a hand-written "FREE PETS" sign. Thousands of exhausted runners detour just to touch his head before finishing. He's been doing it for years, and the 2026 race produced another round of clips of strangers crying-happy mid-race. It's the purest version of the "dog as emotional infrastructure" trend — no tricks, no staging, just a dog absorbing human stress.

This is the single easiest thing to shoot this week.
- **Platform fit:** TikTok (primary) / Reels / RED

- **Why it's hot:** K-pop fandom + rescue-dog adoption is a compounding trend — idol pets get massive cross-platform pickup, and the "tiny dog, huge chaos" contrast is a proven clip format.

Make a RED carousel titled "明星在晒一公斤的领养狗，我家有七公斤的普通混血狗" (celebrities are showing off their 1kg rescue dog; I have a 7kg ordinary mixed breed). Show the unglamorous reality: Fulai's snoring, one crooked ear, the way he waits by the door. The point lands softly: an ordinary mixed breed is just as much a headline at home. Keep it warm — no lecture, no "adopt don't shop" slogan in the caption.
- **Platform fit:** RED (primary) / Instagram Reels

### Story 3: "4 minutes, 68 shelter dogs, before and after" — the transformation format is still undefeated
- **Source:** https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1hSvczhEBT/
- **Why it's hot:** ~976K views and 230K likes on Bilibili; the before/after rescue glow-up is one of the most consistently watched formats in Chinese pet content, and it resurfaces every few months with new compilations.
- **The story:** A creator cut together 68 shelter dogs' intake photos against footage of them months later — matted and shut-down on one side, glossy and sprawled on a sofa on the other. The comments are full of people confessing they cried at minute two. The emotional engine isn't the rescue itself; it's seeing the *end state* — the boring, safe, well-fed ordinary life the dog ends up living.
- **🎬 Fulai Angle:** Everyone films the transformation. Nobody films the boring part after. Make a slow 30s piece: "这是'被爱之后'的第七年" (this is year seven of 'after being loved'). No before photo, no backstory — just Fulai doing nothing on a Tuesday: stretching, drinking water, sighing, falling asleep in a sun patch. Caption closes the loop: every rescue video is trying to get to this frame. It's a fresher, quieter angle than another before/after, and it uses Fulai's actual advantage — he's 7, settled, and unbothered.
- **Platform fit:** RED / TikTok
