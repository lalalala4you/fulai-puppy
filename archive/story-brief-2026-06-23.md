### Story 1: Cooper the Labrador Waited Days in the Same Spot After His Cat Friend Disappeared
- **Source:** https://www.thedodo.com/daily-dodo/loyal-dog-sits-in-the-same-spot-each-day-hoping-his-missing-friend-returns
- **Why it's hot:** Interspecies friendship stories always melt hearts — an 8-year-old dog refusing to eat dinner because he's too busy watching for his missing kitten friend is pure gold for short-form video.
- **The story:** Cooper, an 8-year-old Labrador, reluctantly bonded with a rambunctious new kitten named Winston. They became inseparable — napping in the sun together and exploring the farm side by side. When Winston went missing, Cooper sat in their usual sunny spot for hours each day, scanning the property line. He even skipped dinner (his favorite thing!) just to keep watch. After four agonizing days, Winston returned on his own — and their reunion was pure joy. Now they're even closer than before.

### Story 2: Dog Missing for 12 Years Found — Owner Thought the Call Was a Scam
- **Source:** https://www.thedodo.com/daily-dodo/woman-gets-call-saying-her-dog-was-found-after-12-years-and-thinks-its-a-scam
- **Why it's hot:** Impossible reunions are the most viral animal stories — a 17-year-old Pomeranian named Sparkles found 12 years later is the kind of "never give up hope" narrative that stops the scroll.
- **The story:** In February, Katie Boada got a voicemail saying her dog Sparkles, who went missing in 2014, had been found. She thought it was fraud — but it was real. A good Samaritan found the 17-year-old Pomeranian wandering the streets and brought her to a no-kill shelter. The shelter scanned her microchip and tracked down Katie. Despite 12 years apart, Sparkles recognized her family immediately. The senior pup had health issues (kidney failure, anemia), but Katie took her in without hesitation: "When I took her in, I took the bad and the good."
- **🎬 Fulai Angle:** Use this as a "reminder" style video. Film Fulai getting his ID tag/microchip scanned at the vet with the caption: "This little chip is your ticket home if we ever get separated 🥺." It's educational, emotional, and promotes responsible pet ownership — perfect for Fulai's warm brand. End with a close-up of Fulai getting chin scratches.
- **Platform fit:** TikTok / RED / Reels

### Story 3: Terrified Rescue Dog Transformed by One Simple Sweater
- **Source:** https://www.thedodo.com/daily-dodo/rescuer-gives-terrified-dog-one-simple-piece-of-clothing-and-everything-changes
- **Why it's hot:** Before-and-after rescue transformations never get old — and the twist (a dog sweater being the key to unlocking his personality) makes this especially shareable.
- **The story:** On a rainy night, Lori Carter found a bald, skinny, terrified dog named Goofy cowering on her porch. He was a "shell of a dog" — no barking, no playing, no clue how to be a dog. He spent all his time huddled in a crate. On a whim, Carter bought him a sweater. When she put it on him, everything changed. He wagged his tail for the first time, followed her around the house, and started acting like a real dog. Four years later, Goofy is 40 pounds, still obsessed with his sweaters, and won't let anyone take them off. "It's so crazy that such a small thing could completely change a dog."

Use the caption: "Rescue dogs know warmth when they feel it 🧡" — then cut to Fulai looking cozy and content. Alternatively, ask mom if Fulai has a favorite piece of clothing or accessory and build a short "How one sweater changed everything" story using clips. Mixed breed rescue energy = Fulai energy.
