## 📖 Story #1: Big Mac — The Dog Who Figured Out the McDonald's Drive-Thru

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Source** | [The Dodo — Stray Dog Learns How McDonald's Drive-Thru Works And Convinces Every Car To Order Him Food](https://www.thedodo.com/daily-dodo/stray-dog-learns-how-mcdonalds-drive-thru-works-and-convinces-every-car-to-order-him) |
| **By** | Mary Schwager |
| **Vibe** | 🍔 Smart dog, underdog wins, food = love |

### Why It's Hot 🔥

This story has **everything** TikTok loves: a clever street-smart dog, an unlikely location (McDonald's drive-thru!), a rescue happy ending, and the dog's name is literally **Big Mac**. The rescue video is already going viral on social media. The hook — "dog figures out how the drive-thru works and orders himself a Big Mac" — is basically engineered for the For You Page.

### The Story

For nearly two years, a German shepherd mix roamed the streets of Cleveland, Texas, dodging rescuers. Dog rescuer Zully Vasquez Ventura kept trying to catch him, but he was too scared to trust anyone. Then a McDonald's opened nearby — and this dog *leveled up*.

He figured out that cars at the drive-thru speaker = food. He'd follow cars, bark at windows, and get people to throw him food. When Ventura pulled up to order, the dog made direct eye contact, then turned his face toward the ordering screen — as if to say, "Yeah, I'll take a Big Mac, thanks." She ordered him his first Big Mac, and that became his name. 🥹

A rescue stepped in after her videos went viral. Big Mac is now in a foster home, vetted, and up for adoption. He's 2 years old, 55 pounds, and loves people, dogs, and — you guessed it — McDonald's.

> *"The moral of the story is to give scared dogs a chance; they just need a chance."* — Zully Vasquez Ventura

### Why It's Hot 🔥

This story has racked up millions of views across TikTok and is the definition of **wholesome core**. A dog named Never climbs over his balcony divider EVERY DAY to visit his neighbor Mauro — who, in a twist of cosmic luck, happens to run a pet supply business. The videos of Never getting steam-brushed, playing with enrichment toys, and napping on Mauro's floor are pure serotonin. The TikTok series (@mauro.tutzo) is still ongoing and gaining steam daily.

### The Story

Mauro Tutzo noticed new neighbors moving in next door. Before he could introduce himself, *their dog introduced himself first* — by climbing over the shared balcony divider and staring through Mauro's sliding glass door. Turns out Mauro sells pet supplies from home. Never had hit the jackpot.

Now it's a daily ritual: Never comes over, Mauro gives him enrichment toys, steam-brush spa sessions, dental chew toys. Never naps for hours. When Never's parents come home, he hops back over the balcony. When they went away for two days, Mauro said the building "felt empty."
