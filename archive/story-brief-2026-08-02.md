### Story 1: Little Boy Sets Up Snack Stand to Buy His Disabled Dog a New Wheelchair — $23K Raised
- **Source:** https://dogtime.com/news/226369-little-boy-disabled-dog-discomfort-wheelchair-snacks-sale
- **Why it's hot:** A dyslexic little boy named Roan, who couldn't stand seeing his disabled dog Sammy struggle, set up a homemade snack stand in the front yard — and what happened next has the entire internet sobbing into their phones.
- **The story:** At first glance, it looked like any kid's lemonade stand on a suburban lawn. Then you read the handwritten sign: snacks and drinks for one dollar — raising money for his disabled dog. Roan, a young boy with dyslexia, had noticed his mom Ruth was struggling to afford a new wheelchair for their dog Sammy. Without anyone asking or suggesting, Roan took matters into his own small hands. He sat outside for two hours with Sammy resting beside him under a tree. Nobody stopped. Nobody bought anything. But his mom, filming from the front door through tears, told him "it's the thought that counts."

Then the internet found the video. What happened next is the kind of thing that restores your faith in humanity. Thousands of people asked how they could help. A fundraiser was launched and over $23,000 poured in from strangers around the world. Sammy received a brand-new wheelchair from a longtime supporter, and the family announced that all extra money would be donated to Churchill Animal Protection Society (CAPS), the rescue Sammy originally came from. The full-circle beauty of it — a rescue dog, saved by a little boy who himself knows what it's like to struggle (Roan has been going through years of dyslexia tutoring with Sammy by his side), now paying that love forward to other dogs still waiting for homes. As Roan's sign says in imperfect but perfect spelling: "snaks and drincks for 1$ for disabald dog."
