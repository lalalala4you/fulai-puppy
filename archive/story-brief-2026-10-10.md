### Story 1: Husky Mix Walks Herself to Doggy Daycare — And Acts Like It's Totally Normal
- **Source:** https://www.usatoday.com/story/pets-animals/dog/2026/04/22/dog-shows-up-alone-to-doggy-daycare-and-goes-viral/89738642007/
- **Why it's hot:** A mixed-breed dog who loves routine so much she took herself to daycare is peak "dogs are people" comedy-warmth — the internet can't resist a confident dog with a plan.
- **The story:** Missy, a 5-year-old husky mix, is a Monday-to-Friday regular at Spot's Place in Regina, Saskatchewan. One Monday her mom hadn't booked her in — so Missy simply walked the 5–10 minutes from home and strolled in on her own. Staff were stunned: "She showed up like, 'Hey guys, I'm here! Sorry I'm late!'" ### Story 2: The Viral TikTok Trend Where People Tell Their Pet's Life Story in Milestones
- **Source:** https://www.aol.com/articles/viral-tiktok-trend-people-sharing-122621000.html
- **Why it's hot:** A fresh (early October 2026) trend where people caption photos of their pets with the milestones they lived through together — and the comment sections are turning into a giant, emotional pet-story thread.
- **The story:** The format is simple: "X years, X apartments/moves/jobs, X heartbreaks, X graduations" next to a photo of your pet, ending on how they were there for all of it. People are posting everything from a 21-year bond to "1 year, from a cold field to a cozy apartment" for a rescued puppy. It works because it reframes a pet as a witness to your whole life — happy, funny, and quietly devastating all at once.
- **🎬 Fulai Angle:** "7 years. 1 Fulai. Every good thing that came home." A slideshow of Fulai across the years, each slide captioned with a milestone ("the year we moved", "the year it rained for a month", "the year mom cried and Fulai sat on her foot"). ### Story 3: Braveheart the Rescue Stray Wins the Internet With His Big Head
